# Vision — Overa (اورا)

## What it is
Overa is a Persian (RTL) peer-to-peer rental marketplace: people rent
out items they own (cameras, tools, camping gear, audio equipment,
sports gear, ...) to others for short periods. Think "Airbnb for
things" but localized for Iran: Persian UI, Persian digits, Rial/Toman
pricing, Iranian cities, Shaba/IBAN banking.

## Who it's for
- Renters (مستأجر): find and book items nearby, pay a deposit held in
  escrow, receive delivery, return item, get deposit back.
- Owners (میزبان): list items, manage bookings, receive payouts,
  handle damage claims.
- Platform (اورا): trust, escrow, identity verification, dispute
  resolution.

## Core value
Access over ownership: "وسایل بهتر، زندگی سبک‌تر" — better gear,
lighter life. Trust first: escrow deposits, verified identity, insurance
badges, transparent fees.

## Brand feel
Luminous lavender atmosphere, soft violet accents, rounded tactile
surfaces, high-trust signaling. Persian Vazirmatn typography. Not cold
classifieds; curated, welcoming, safe.
